## Behavior

- Town-wide `MuseumBook` with 4-bit donator ids per fossil (25) / art (15) / fish (40) / insect (40). Values 1–4 = player, 5 = deleted player still on display.
- `MuseumDonation.donate` writes the bit after a successful hand-over (`mMmd_RequestMuseumDisplay`). Wings rebuild on next enter — not live from the entrance.
- Fossil wing: `rom_museum2` shell; donated → real `int_din_*`; empty → species dummy pedestal at absolute `mMmd_UT` cells on the 16×16 acre (shell keeps acre NW at world origin — `Interior.bind` uses origin 0 for museum rooms). Most din parts are `aFTR_SHAPE_TYPEC` (2×2) so `furniture_world` adds +½ cell like `aMR_UnitNumber2Position`; stego head / ptera are TYPEB; solos are TYPEA. Fossil hosts get AABB box colliders.
- Entrance floor is 10×8 from cell (1,3) (`rom_museum1`); painting / fossil floors are 14×12 from (1,1). Painting/fossil shell walls sit on the outer floor cell (north strip ≈ one cell thick); `InteriorBuilder` insets those walls one cell. Entrance / fish / insect keep walls on the floor AABB — fish/insect exits at z=560 sit on the south rim, and inset put a wall face on the door. Door sensors cut wall gaps (entrance has north×2 + west + east + south; half-width 40 GX, the two `DOOR` units every doorway is in `collision[]`) with porch slabs so openings stay walkable. Painting mid walls at unit Z=5 / Z=9 get E–W partition colliders with gaps where `ART_CELLS` has no hang.
- Painting wing: `rom_museum3` shell (`BLOCK_COMBI_ROM_MUSEUM3` — not museum2); donated → `obj_artNN`; empty → `obj_art_dummy*`. Host at Y=40 GX (`aMP_DrawOneArt`); origin-snap so the frame bottom sits on that hang line (pipeline verts start ~10 GX above local 0). Keep authored mesh XZ (wall depth) — do not AABB-recenter onto the cell. ART02/ART03 forgeries cannot donate. Museum canvas CI4 hashes differ from house FTR; ACHD falls back to `int_sum_art*` / `int_ike_art*` twins so walls get HD paintings. Nameplates / frames (`*_name_tex`, `*_gaku_tex`) skip ACHD (hash collisions pull scrap-board sheets) and decode with house wood pals (sum) or museum `*_etc_pal` (ike ang/sya/fel). Empty `obj_art_dummy*` (except dummy03) ship neon CI4 in REL — convert twins them to dummy03 wood / ACHD. Draw path is POLY_OPA — runtime materials use scissor/opaque depth, not soft BLEND.
- Fish wing: donated species swim in `mfish_group_tbl` tanks at `suisou_pos`, with `mfish_init_data` scale/`_0C` swim height and museum `act_mus_*` cKF. Tank glass: four `obj_suisou1` + sea `obj_museum5`, bottoms on floor, box colliders. Pipeline `ckf_basis` stands the chain on +Y and rolls dorsal to −X; `MuseumFishVisual.SWIM_FROM_STAND` maps nose→+Z and dorsal→+Y before swim yaw (crayfish also +180° Y per `mfish_zarigani_dw`). Glass XLU is three combiners, not a flat wash: `evw` is `ENV(0,30,120) + TEXEL0×SHADE` with `G_TEXTURE_GEN` (`tank_glass.gdshader`; small-tank prim alpha 30 / lod 10, sea 60 / 20), `mizu` is `TEXEL0×vertex` with alpha `90 + texel×(50/256)`, and the caustic is `ENV(150,255,255)` with alpha `(water1+water2)×vertex` — emu64's second cycle samples tile 1 — scrolling `{1,-1}` / `{-1,1}` after the evw Y flip at `game_frame/8` texels (`tank_caustic.gdshader`). Fish, plants and the player use `field_lit.gdshader`. The light sum is the 8-bit palette fraction (`Color8` is byte/255, not a second sRGB curve) times emu64's GX term: directional lights are `color * clamp(n·L)` with attenuation off, and point lights are `color * (1-(d/r)²) * clamp(n·L)`. The room lamp is `(100,120,130)` radius 1000 at `(320,220,320)`. Two extra cyan lights `(0,150,255)` track the player onto the tank in front of them (`MuseumFishLight.glow_gx`, fade 60 GX, max radius 55).
- Insect wing: donated species at museum anchors; `active_time_tbl` / `relax_time_tbl` gate motion; pose flap continues slowly while relaxed.
- Plaques list donated members of each group (`Museum_*_Set_Msg*Info`).
- Catch reports use the shorter already-collected line once the species is in the museum (`mSM_CHECK_LAST_FISH_GET`).
- Hours 9–17 on museum rooms; save/load via `Game.to_save()["museum"]`.
- Camera: museum rooms follow the player at Camera2 distance 620 — not home pin/frame (`Camera2_InDoorCheck` is homes only).
- Doors: no E prompt anywhere. Outdoor museum (`aMsm_check_player`): fires with the player's centre within √1100 GX of the +100 GX stand **and** facing the door within 45°; the player then walks `INTO_S1` under a triforce (`IRIS`) wipe. Outdoor→entrance spawn is `aMsm_museum_enter_data` `{240,0,440}` facing north, standing (not scene player `{240,0,200}`, no walk-in).
- Indoor doors are the FG `DOOR` / `EXIT_DOOR` warp units (FG templates 383–387): `Player_actor_check_nextgoto` fires once the player's **own unit** is one of them, so each trigger box is exactly those two 40 GX units and `Door` checks the player's centre, not the reach probe. Entrance: painting ut (3..4,1), fossil (7..8,1), insect (1,6..7), fish (10,6..7), leave `EXIT_DOOR` (5..6,12). Wings: painting/fossil (7..8,14), insect (14,13..14), fish (1,13..14). Spawns follow `MUSEUM_*_door_data`.
- Wing links (`Player_actor_set_nextgoto_info_type1`): the player brakes to a stand (`INVADE`, `WAIT1`), the screen fades (`door_data` wipe type 2 — `WIPE_TYPE_FADE_WHITE`, but every wipe is coloured with `setcolor(0)`, so it is black), and `FADE_TYPE_OTHER_ROOM` keeps the BGM running. The entrance leave uses `structure_exit_door_data` (triforce) and the outdoor emerge is `GO_OUT_S1` (`extra_data` 3).
- Entrance wing map (facing into the hall from the south exit): **N-west** painting (`rom_museum3`) · **N-east** fossil (`rom_museum2`) · **W** insect (`rom_museum4`) · **E** fish (`rom_museum5`). Shell ids come from `field_data.c` `BLOCK_COMBI_ROM_MUSEUM*`; door destinations from `MUSEUM_ENTRANCE_door_data`.
- **Blathers dialogue** (`blathers_greeting.json`, `museum_dialogue.gd`, `blathers_trivia.json`): nocturnal schedule (drowsy `aCR_SLEEP_TIME_START`/`_END` 06:00–18:00, holding the normal wait pose for 6s before the `npc_1_wait_nemu1` sleep pose, only on the awake→drowsy edge; "wake with a start" first talk of the day), one-time orientation monologue with a "shall I stop?" choice, daily-repeat variants, and a Talk menu (Donate / About / Leave). Blathers stands at `museum_entrance_actable` ut (6,5) + curator `+20` GX on X → `(280,0,220)`, facing south; owl face flaps while uttering; talk/donate through the dialogue overlay with `CAMERA2_PROCESS_TALK` framing.
- **Donation opens the pockets** (`mMmd` IV_OPEN, modelled on the intro-payment flow): Blathers' "Donate" choice / the pocket `DONATE` interaction sets `Game.pocket_requests.donation_pending` and opens the inventory overlay; every item worth offering shows a **Donate** tag (`Inventory.tags_for_slot`). Picking one calls `PocketRequests.take_donation` (commit + `donation_result`), closes the pockets, and Blathers responds via `MuseumDialogue.build_outcome`: examine line → species trivia → completion fanfare (per-skeleton lecture / per-collection / whole museum) → "anything else?" (yes reopens the pockets). `MuseumDonation.donate()` removes the item from inventory unconditionally on accept. Rejections (already-donated per category, bug "release it OUT of doors" panic, non-category decline, forgery, unexamined-fossil → Farway referral) play the full `donate_act[]` GET+RETURN sequence — `HandOver.player_offers_npc_rejects()`: player extends the item, Blathers takes and examines it (`NPC_GET_PULL_WAIT`), then hands it back (`NPC_GET_RETURN`) — distinct from the accept path's single put-away hand-over. A raw, unidentified `fossil` item is refused outright and handed back (`ac_npc_curator_move.c_inc`'s `donate_act[3]`) — there is no on-the-spot identification in the original either; it has to go through the Post Office first. Donating one piece of a multi-part fossil skeleton that doesn't complete the set gets its own "pieces remaining" line (`aCR_chk_fossil_parts_complete`), distinct from a solo donation's "thanks". Closing the pockets without picking cancels. Headless / no-UI falls back to `build_donate`'s dialogue-list picker.
- Committed trivia is a faithful re-expression (own wording, real natural-history facts). A local, unversioned verbatim bank at `assets/generated/dialogue/overrides/` overrides `blathers_greeting` / `blathers_trivia` / donation nodes by id.
- **Fossil identification** (`fossil_catalog.gd` + `data/fossils.json`, `travel_book.gd`): 25 identified fossils as donatable/sellable `FurnitureData`. The identification path is mailing a raw fossil to the "Museum" address-book contact (`mPr_CheckMuseumAddress`, `mMsm_SendResultMail`); `TravelBook` models that scheduling. Two entry points reach `TravelBook.queue_fossil()`: the Post Office's "send N fossils at once" batch shortcut, and writing a letter addressed to "Museum" with a raw fossil attached as a gift. Either way, the next 06:00 renew returns a `RECV_PRESENT` letter per fossil (random identity) plus a one-time intro letter on first dig, delivered to `Inventory._mail` and readable at the house **mailbox** (`scenes/world/mailbox.tscn`) or the Letters pocket page. Mailbox interaction matches `ac_mailbox_move.c_inc`'s `aMBX_pl_open`/`_pl_close`: `mailbox.gd::interact()` awaits the lid clip, opens the Letters page seeded to the last-used index, awaits the page closing, then plays the lid closed. Flag state follows `Inventory.mail_changed`.
- **Redd** (`redd_book.gd`, `features/shops/redd/redd.gd`): unlocks after the museum owns any art or the town is 14 days old; tent open one seeded weekday/week; 4-slot stock of genuine + forged paintings (ART02/03 always forged) plus the odd furniture piece, one purchase per visit. Forged art is item id `art_forgery_NN` (`MuseumBook.display_info_for_item` rejects it → Blathers' `forgery` branch). Goods are bought off the tent floor (`ShopStock` → Redd's `offer_item`), reading `Game.redd.stock()`. The `broker_shop` room and Redd NPC are wired; outdoor tent placement in the world generator is not yet in `WorldGenerator`.
- **Lighting**: window beams from the scene actor tables, placed by `MuseumPresenter.present_sunshine` with `police_sunshine.tscn` (`MuseumDisplay.*_SUNSHINE_GX`). The entrance hall has four `ef_room_sunshine_museum` actors (`{123,0,180}`, `{360,0,180}`, `{123,0,380}`, `{360,0,380}`, args 2 / 3 alternating west / east) drawing `obj_museum1_shine`: a shaft coloured by the stained-glass texture (`TEXEL0 × ENV_ALPHA 180 + PRIM × 75`, alpha `TEXEL0 × TEXEL1 fade × PRIM_LOD_FRAC`, `museum_sunshine.gdshader`) and a stained-glass floor patch (`TEXEL0 × PRIM`). The insect wing has two `ef_room_sunshine_minsect` actors (`{40,0,280}` / `{520,0,280}`) drawing `obj_museum4_shine` with the police box combiner. Both effects use the police box's timing, alpha, anchor X (−2 west, +0 east) and mirrored east beam, without its camera cull; the entrance's Y is `1 + BgY − 40` like the police box, the insect wing's is the actor's own 0 (one GX lower). Fish wing gets fourteen `obj_museum5_kusa*` at `kusa_pos` plus the `hasu` lily at `{420,0,220}`, and `museum_bubbles.gd` (twenty `ef_suisou_awa` vents). `interior.gd::_apply_museum_mood` drops museum ambient to 0.72 with a per-wing tint. `museum_clock.gd` turns the entrance clock hands to the RTC. Wall ceiling darkening is baked vertex shade (`Vtx.cn[]` with `G_LIGHTING` off on `rom_museum*` DLs), not a shader.
- **Lighting**: `obj_museum1_shine` (entrance) / `obj_museum4_shine` (insect) god-ray meshes via `VisualStructureMaterials.apply_light_shaft_surface` (unshaded XLU, no depth write, emission bloom, alpha scaled by clock daylight). Fish wing gets fourteen `obj_museum5_kusa*` at `kusa_pos` plus the `hasu` lily at `{420,0,220}`, and `museum_bubbles.gd` (twenty `ef_suisou_awa` vents). `interior.gd::_apply_museum_mood` drops museum ambient to 0.72 with a per-wing tint. `museum_clock.gd` turns the entrance clock hands to the RTC. Wall ceiling darkening is baked vertex shade (`Vtx.cn[]` with `G_LIGHTING` off on `rom_museum*` DLs), not a shader.
- Static entrance fixtures are authored nodes, not imperative builds: `Blathers` (`museum_blathers.gd`), `MuseumClock` (`museum_clock.gd`), and `LightShaft` (`museum_light_shaft.gd`) live under `museum_entrance.tscn` `Furniture/` with baked transforms; the insect wing's `LightShaft` is authored in `museum_insect.tscn`. `InteriorBuilder.populate_authored` keeps any `Furniture/*` child named in `AUTHORED_FIXTURE_NAMES` (or group `authored_fixture`) across a re-populate — only the donation-driven exhibits (`present_fossils` / `present_paintings` / `present_fish` / `present_insects`) rebuild.
- Entrance stained glass: XLU `rom_museum1_modelT` (`*_mado*_tex`); `mado_pal` RGB5A3 glass colors ship with A=0, revived so panes keep color. Entrance floor clock is authored `Furniture/MuseumClock` at world `(12,0,7.5)`; hands (`hari_*`) turn to the RTC each frame.
- Insect programs are museum-idle orbits/sways, not a full port of every `minsect_*` overlay. Tank grass and the lily play their cKF sway (the extra joint flutter in `kusa_before_disp` is not applied). Bubbles follow `ef_suisou_awa` (scale 0.00125–0.005, rise to GX 115); a large bubble in a small tank leaves an `ef_turi_hamon` ring. Redd's forgery "tells" are a display-name marker + low sell price, not a per-painting visual perturbation. Jellyfish has no `act_mus_*` mesh in the pipeline (decomp skeleton is NULL). Empty painting frames use `obj_art_dummy*` when present.
- Not modelled: the player's own walk-up/hop into place before the mailbox lid opens (`aMBX_pl_wait` → `mPlayer_INDEX_MAIL_JUMP`); villager/event/bank letters; a "museum complete" mail (`mMsm_SetCompMail`); the 40-entry insect-only extra trivia table (no reference text to draw from); the accept path's GET-demo/PUTAWAY-demo split (decomp plays a hand-over before the examine line and a separate one after the trivia line — this port plays one hand-over up front and removes the item at commit time).

## Collision and exhibit talk (decomp-checked)

- **Collision is the BG unit map, nothing else.** `mFM_bg_data_c.collision[]` for `rom_museum1`–`5` (all in `bg_data.c`; `rom_museum4`, the insect wing, has no model in its row, so the pipeline names it from `BG_TYPE_*`). Any unit higher than the floor (4 counts) is a wall to the player (`mCoBG_CheckHeightExactly`: more than 3 GX up). `InteriorShellBuilder.add_museum_unit_collision` builds a floor under the whole 16×16 grid plus `InteriorUnitCollision` hulls on every non-floor unit, replacing the old inner-rect perimeter, door gaps/porches and painting partitions. The exhibit actors have no `ClObj`, but `mMmd_MuseumFossilProcess_MakeFgData` writes all 25 fossil slots into the fossil wing's FG as furniture (the fossil, or its dummy pedestal before donation), so every fossil footprint is solid (`MuseumFossilCol`). Paintings, insect cases and tanks add nothing beyond the BG. Blathers is the NPC pipe (`aNPC_CoInfoData`: radius 20 GX, height 30 GX).
- **Exhibit talk** (`MuseumPlaque`, one node per talk point; no prompt off the point):
  - Fossils (`aMF_fossil_data_table`, 25 points): within 42.5 GX of the unit centre, facing the exhibit's side within ±22.5° (`mRmTp_CheckTalkAble`; diagonals never count). The player turns toward the point (`1−√0.7`, 0x800).
  - Paintings (`aMP_CheckTalkAbleDist`): (x·40+60, z·40+40) GX, within 33 GX, facing north; turns due north.
  - Insect cases / fish tanks (`*_Check_Talk_Distance`): the sign's ±20 GX square, facing north within 67.5°; turns due north (0.3, 22.5°).
  - Text: the disc's 12191 (donor is a resident), 12192 (former resident), 12193 (empty); cases and tanks page through their donated pieces with 12194 / 12195 ("Read" / "Quit reading"). Cream window (255,255,205), no nameplate.

## Tank fish (decomp-ported base)

- `MuseumFishActor` is the shared base of `ac_museum_fish_base`, run in the decomp's units (GX, radians) with every ease taking `ticks`. It covers the normal burst, turn, dummy coast (drift side-on to the camera), peck and ground-peck processes, `mfish_base_FishMove` (60–110 GX band), `mfish_move_smooth` along the glass, `mfish_get_flow_vec` (per-tank swirl plus the nearest fish's wake), `Museum_Fish_BGCheck` (the `objchk_pos` point inside `54 + _28·f` / the sea tank's lobes) and `Museum_Fish_ObjBGCheck` (each small tank's rock/driftwood corner).
- `MuseumFishSchool` is the room update: `mfish_get_player_area` every 30 frames (walking up to a tank wakes its fish), the per-tank current angle (`_14daa`), `Museum_Fish_Object_Check` (fish shove apart by `_20`) and `Museum_Fish_BigFishObjCheck` (tank 3 gives way to the arapaima and giant snakehead). Peck: piranhas come to a player at their tank, carp/koi when the player carries candy, goldfish when they carry fruit.
- Draw is `mfish_onefish_dw`: yaw, then pitch and roll (−½ the turn left, ±20°) about a pivot at `0.25·ofs_y` / `1.5·ofs_z`, back `0.5·ofs_z` — the body sits `ofs_z` along its heading; `ofs_y` is a pivot, not a lift.
- Not yet ported: the 17 per-species handlers (`mfish_ct`/`mv`/`dw` tables) — koi, catfish, bass, big fish, small fish, loach, stringfish, sea fish, goldfish, arowana, eel, goby, guppy, coelacanth, crawfish, frog/lily pad, killifish, jellyfish, red snapper. Every species currently swims on the base.
