# Post office & police box

Research notes from [ACreTeam/ac-decomp](https://github.com/ACreTeam/ac-decomp). Behavioral reference only.

## Godot

`PostBook` / `PoliceBook` on `Game`. Authored rooms `post_office.tscn` / `police_box.tscn` under `scenes/world/interiors/`. Layout tables: `PostDisplay`, `PoliceDisplay`. Police talk: `PoliceTalk` (Booker, `booker_talk.json`), `CopperTalk` (Copper, `copper_talk.json`). Actors: `booker.tscn` (interior), `copper.tscn` (outdoors, placed by `WorldGenerator._place_copper`), `police_sunshine.tscn` (window beams). Play through outdoor enter → `interior.tscn` mounts the room and calls `populate()`. F6 on the room `.tscn` alone uses `public_room`’s standalone preview (camera + light + populate).

## Behavior

- **Post:** shell `grd_post_office`, outdoor enter GX `{160,0,300}` north (`aPOFF_post_office_door_data`; not scene player `{100,0,200}`), exit `EXIT_DOOR` (3,8)/(4,8), Pelly (`pga_1` / `SP_NPC_POST_GIRL`) 7–19 / Phyllis (`pgb_1` / `SP_NPC_POST_GIRL2`) otherwise at stand GX `{160,0,100}`, letter piles from `mPO_get_keep_mail_sum` at X 80…240 / Y60 / Z60. Desk hull + Talk at GX `{160,0,140}` (shell mesh has no physics). eTM `PTerminal` at GX `{60,0,240}` (`msg_15854` → GBA missing → `msg_15859`). Clerk talk uses imported bank `msg_*` from `aPG_set_talk_info` (fallback `post_girl_greeting.json`). Desk choices: Mail / Deposit-or-Repay / Save → follow-up menus (`PostUse`). Always enterable (lights only). Do not use `pla`/`plb` — those are Booker / Pete.
- **Police:** shell `police_indoor`, outdoor enter GX `{200,0,380}` north (`aPBOX_police_box_enter_data`; not scene player `{200,0,400}`), exit `EXIT_DOOR1` (4,10)/(5,10), Booker (`pla_1` / `SP_NPC_POLICE2`) starts at `{180,0,260}` (actable ut (4,6)). `plc_1` is outdoor Copper.
  - **Storage** (`m_police_box.c`): 20 `keep_items`. `mPB_police_box_init` runs once in `m_start_data_init` (new town): 1 ABC furniture + 2 ABC shirts, never re-run when the box empties. `mPB_keep_item` accepts ITEM1/FTR, writes at index `keep_item_sum()` (a claimed gap is not refilled in place), and when full drops slot 0 and shifts. `mPB_copy_itemBuf` packs the list; Booker calls it when the player steps on `EXIT_DOOR1`. `mPB_keep_all_item_in_block` takes a batch of ≤ 20 (extra items overwrite random batch slots), dropping old items from the front to fit.
  - **06:00 top-up** (`mPB_force_set_keep_item`, once per renewal in `mAGrw_RenewalFgItem`): only with ≤ 5 kept, `qrand() >= 0` (50%). Category roll 0–99 `<=` {85 goods, 90 tool/sapling, 95 flower bag, 100 umbrella}; goods roll `<=` {35 furniture, 58 stationery, 88 clothing, 94 carpet, 100 wallpaper} from the common list; tools = net/axe/shovel/rod/sapling/cedar sapling; bags = `WHITE_PANSY_BAG + (int)(fqrand()*8)` (never the ninth).
  - **Shelves**: the `police_indoor` BG (`police_indoor.col.json`) raises the three shelf rows 20 GX, plus the phone desk and locker. `PolicePresenter` builds solid hulls from those units (`PoliceDisplay.raised_units`), so the player walks round the shelves and kept items rest on the tops. Back-wall clock `obj_clock_koban` at GX `{200,0,30}` (`aHC_position_data`).
  - **Display** (`bg_police_item`): each `RSV_POLICE_ITEM_N` (0xF128+N) unit in `FG_TYPE_POLICE_INDOOR` (0xCE) shows `keep_items[N]` as its field card (`mNT_get_itemTableNo`) at the unit centre, `mCoBG_GetBgY_OnlyCenter_FromWpos2` height, scale 0.01. Godot reads the units from the FG catalog; without it, a fallback table (rows z 1/3/5).
  - **Booker** (`ac_npc_police2`): actions WAIT / WALK / RUN (same zone or other zone) / TURN / CHECK_ANSWER / TALK_END_WAIT, plus GREET when entered by door. Zones: unit z clamped 2…6 (5 rows), unit x split `>1 / >4 / >7` (4 columns); waypoints X {60,140,260,340}, Z {100,140,180,220,300}. Decide: dist² < 2450 → WAIT; other zone → next zone via `aPOL2_get_next_zone` (rows 1/3 are shelf rows, `RANDOM(2)` for same-row moves), walk if dist² < 5000 else run; same zone → walk/run in place facing the player. Speeds walk 1.0 (acc 0.1 / dec 0.2), run 4.0 (0.4 / 0.8); `chase_angle` 11.25°; > 90° off → TURN. Collision with the player → WAIT (Godot: the 50 GX stop covers it).
  - **Talk**: walk-in SPEAK 0x0784 (empty) / 0x0785 (items, sets `_99C`); A-talk 0x077D (items) / 0x0786 (empty) / 0x0787 (empty after `_99C`); face kept item + A → SPEAK 0x077E with `ITEM_STR0` (name + article) → CHOICE0: `mPlib_Get_space_putin_item` (tickets: `_forTICKET` stacking) → full: continue 0x0781; else pocket write, `RSV_NO` FG poke (redraw), `NA_SE_ITEM_GET`. He turns to the player before the window opens and the player is held in demo wait meanwhile.
  - **Window sunshine** (`ef_room_sunshine_police`): actors at `{40,0,200}` (arg 2, left) and `{360,0,200}` (arg 3, right); anchor X −1 / +1 net, Y `1 + BgY − 40`. Left beam 00–04 (4 h span) and 12–20 (8 h), right beam 04–12 and 20–24, X scale `1.5·sin(sec·90°/span)`. Alpha 120→0 over 00–04, peak 255 at noon, 0→120 over 20–24; rain/snow × 0.6; × `windowlight_alpha`, which in the police box (no light switch) targets 1 only 05:00–18:00 and 0 for ±120 s around the `s16`-cast marks (00:00, 12:00, and wrapped 05:47:44), stepping 0.005 per frame. Colour `sun_color_window` 04–20 else `moon_color_window`. Culled when the camera eye is at or beyond its own X side.
  - **Post office sunshine** (`ef_room_sunshine_posthouse`, `obj_yubinkyoku_shine_modelT`): the same effect and `police_sunshine.tscn` with the post office model, actors at `{40,0,160}` / `{280,0,160}`, a 0.05 scale instead of 0.01, Y `1 + BgY` (no −40) and no camera cull.
  - The beam textures load through classic `gsDPSetTextureImage` + `gsDPLoadBlock`, so their texels are N64 row-major, not GX blocks: the police floor patch is four window panes, and the shaft's I4 fades from the top.
  - **Copper** (`ac_npc_police`, `fd_npc_land_actable`): on the police acre, `POLICE_STATION` unit + (2, 0); faces south (`ACT_TURN` back to 0 after talks). Removed on morning-aerobics event days. Keeps whatever FG item is under his unit on spawn. Walk-out greeting 0x0771 (`last_scene_no == SCENE_POLICE_BOX`, `in_initial_block`, once per actor). Menu 0x0772 (<10:00) / 0x0773 (<17:00) / 0x0774 (else) / 0x0775 (<05:00): hint (`aPOL_get_hint_msg_no`: first job 0x2C2D, none 0x2C37, run/today/later tables per `mEv_EVENT_ARTIST…CARPET_PEDDLER`), lost and found (0x0782 with the count / 0x0783), never mind (0x0777). Foreign players get a map option first (`aPOL_check_select2`, 0x18C6–0x18C9 / 0x18CA). Idle: 06:00–07:00, not raining, player > 67.5° off his facing, 5% per think → `TAISOU1/2/7` ×2/4/1 (ends when the player comes into view); 02:00–04:00, player ≥ 60 GX, 5% → `WAIT_NEMU1` ×5 then `GYAFUN1` (ends early if the player comes within 60).
- Outdoor enter: post `door_type 0` + `OPEN1`; police `door_type 1` + `INTO_S1` (already on `StructureDoor`).
- Shells use 16× acre verts (`FieldCatalog.interior_uses_acre_verts`) like shops — not classic `room01` GX scale.
- Letter write uses preset bodies + a recipient picker rather than a full stationery editor. Bank amount entry is choice presets (All / 1,000), not the money keypad overlay. House **loan** lives on `Inventory.loan`; when `loan > 0`, Pelly's Deposit choice is repay, and bank savings unlock once loan is 0. PTerminal plays the exact eTM welcome / choices; GBA link answers with the "not properly connected" bank lines (`msg_15859`). Police: the special-event hint has no visitor acre to name (no visiting NPC is placed in town yet), the April Fools' lines are not wired, the ABC / common priority lists are the plain shop pools, Copper does not leave for aerobics events, and the field callers that turn dropped items in (`mFI_SetFGStructureKeep`, event clean-ups, house moves, snowmen) are not wired — only the 06:00 top-up feeds the box.
